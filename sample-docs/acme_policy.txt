Acme Corp Employee Handbook - Leave and Work Policy

Vacation: Every full-time employee receives 20 vacation days per calendar year.
Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year.

Sick leave: Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is
required after 3 consecutive sick days.

Remote work: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their
manager's approval. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the office's local time.

Office closures: The office is closed on all public holidays and on December 24.

Expenses: Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days. Meals are reimbursed
up to 50 dollars per day when travelling.
